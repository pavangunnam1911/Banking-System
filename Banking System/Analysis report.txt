Analysis Report
1)Create a new bank.
2)people in bank:-manager,staff,account holder.
3)bank users:-
a)Staff can
•	Create new account for holder
•	Account should have:-username ,password
•	Can update,delete  holder account 
•	Add new accepted currency with exchange rate
•	Amount transfer service charges.
•	Service charges can be modified which are preset by bank.
•	Access to account transaction history.
•	Revert transactions
b)Holder can
•	Deposit Money.(Any currency to INR)
•	Withdraw Money.(INR)
•	Transfer Money.(INR)
•	Transactions(Self).
Patterns:-
1.	Bank ID-first 3 letters of bank name + current date.
2.	Account ID- first 3 letters of holder name+current date.
3.	Transaction ID- “TXN”+ bank ID +AccountID+current date.
flow:-
•	Create a Bank as main class.
•	Bank will have staff & holder accounts class with username and password.
•	Staff will initially create account and immediately login and have create holder account functionality.
•	Once holder created account and logged in .he will deposit money .
•	Once deposited he can withdraw or transfer to different accounts.
•	Charges will be applied according to transaction type.
•	Staff can revert any transaction.
•	Staff can update delete account.
